This website

rorakhit.com has no accounts, forms, cookies or ads. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you. Visitors in the EU are excluded from it.

Personal apps (ro-mail-agent and Ori)

ro-mail-agent and Ori are apps I built for my own use. They are not offered to anyone else, and the only Google account they connect to is mine.

  • What they access: read-only access to my own Google Calendar (the calendar.readonly scope). They can't create, change or delete events.
  • How the data is used: to show my schedule in my own daily briefing and to help my personal assistant notice patterns in my week.
  • Where it's stored: on my own computer, encrypted where it's sensitive. Calendar data is processed on my own machines and isn't uploaded to outside services.
  • Sharing: the data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. It isn't used to train AI models.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored data is deleted along with the app.

These apps' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
